# Gas sleeve

A square album sleeve with the nebula as its cover art (red, gold and teal clouds on deep blue) and a record sliding out of the right side, its grooves drawn in periwinkle. The square plus half-disc outline is the classic record-shop pictogram, in our colours.

**At 16 px:** an 11 px colour square with one periwinkle groove arc peeking out to the right. Floats on transparency.

**Weaknesses:** the cover is three soft blobs, which at 16 px looks like a generic gradient tile; the nebula is not legible as a nebula until 180 px. The record's dark body disappears on a dark tab bar, leaving only the arcs. Least tied to the map of the three. The 180 px icon uses feTurbulence on the cover.
