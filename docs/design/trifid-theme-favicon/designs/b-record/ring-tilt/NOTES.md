# Ring tilt

A record seen at a tilt, so it reads as a spiral galaxy or a planet's ring: the grooves run red to gold to teal to blue across the disc and a bright star sits where the label would be. The slanted oval is a silhouette no other tab has.

**At 16 px:** grooves dropped; a slanted oval in a red to teal sweep with a white core and a thin dark underside. Floats on transparency.

**Weaknesses:** the oval fills only about half the 16 px square, so it looks smaller and softer than its neighbours. Without grooves it reads as "galaxy" more than "record". The gold middle is the weakest part on a light tab bar.
