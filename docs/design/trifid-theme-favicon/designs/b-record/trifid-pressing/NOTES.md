# Trifid pressing

A record seen face-on, pressed in three colours of gas: red, gold and teal lobes divided by three swirling dark dust lanes, as in the Trifid Nebula. The spindle hole is a white star.

**At 16 px:** no groove rings; three colour wedges, three 1 px curved lanes and a 2 px white centre on a dark disc. Floats on transparency; the dark rim holds it on a light tab bar and the colour holds it on a dark one.

**Weaknesses:** it is still a circle with a dot, so the colour and the swirl do all the work. The three-part pinwheel can recall a beach ball or other tri-colour round logos. Grooves only show from 32 px up. The 180 px icon uses feTurbulence to wobble the lanes.
