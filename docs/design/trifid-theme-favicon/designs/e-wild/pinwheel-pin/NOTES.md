# Pinwheel pin

**Idea.** A map pin ("explore the map") filled with the five Ember colours as flat, hard-edged swirling blades that spin out from a white star in a dark eye. No softness anywhere: a small heraldic badge, the playful one of the three.

**At 16 px.** Redrawn with four blades (red, gold, deep blue, vivid teal; periwinkle dropped), a 2.5 px white dot in a dark eye, and a thin dark rim. The blue blade fills the point so the pin shape survives on dark bars. Reads clearly as a multicoloured pin on both bars.

**Weaknesses.** A pin says "place" before it says "music", and a multicolour pin can recall map-app logos. The blade curves flatten into near-straight wedges at 16 px, so the swirl is only felt from 32 px up. Flat colour is a deliberate break from the soft gas, which may feel off-theme next to the nebula.
