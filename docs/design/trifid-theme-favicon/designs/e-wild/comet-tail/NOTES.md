# Comet tail

**Idea.** A comet whose head is a star-white core in a gold and red-orange coma, and whose tail is the gas, running vivid teal to periwinkle to deep blue. It floats with no tile: a thin sky-dark rim follows the teardrop so it holds on light and dark tab bars alike.

**At 16 px.** Redrawn as a fatter teardrop: a white head about 4 px across ringed in orange, and a plain teal-to-blue tail (no dust lane). Reads as a comet or a lit match head; warm head against cool tail is the whole signal.

**Weaknesses.** It covers only about half the 16 px box (diagonal shape), so it is physically smaller than a tiled icon. The dark rim goes soft and grey on a light bar. The tail tip dims on dark bars. The 180 px version is glowier than the tab versions, so the two are cousins rather than twins. No direct music cue.
