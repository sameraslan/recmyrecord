# Rec giant

**Idea.** The "rec" in recmyrecord as a record button: the red dot is a red giant star with a gold-white hot spot, a teal-to-blue arc of gas swept round its lower left, and one white companion star where a REC light would blink. Sits on the existing dark rounded tile.

**At 16 px.** An 8 px orange-red ball with a warm highlight on the dark tile, a 1.5 px teal-blue arc under it and a 2 px white square top right. Strong and readable; red on black is the brightest of the three in a tab row.

**Weaknesses.** Still a dark rounded tile with a round thing in it, so the silhouette is the least distinctive. Could be mistaken for a screen-recorder or a planet app. Gold appears only as the highlight and periwinkle barely at all. The soft corona muddies the tile edge slightly at 16 px.
