# Eddy

**Idea.** A whirl of three gas streams (teal, gold, red-orange) curling into one bright core, like the brightest knot in the map. It is the "swirling of the colours" as a mark, on the dark rounded tile.

**At 16 px.** Three fat comma shapes about 3 px wide with shorter sweep than the master, no core star. It reads as a three-colour pinwheel; the dark gaps near the centre are only partly resolved, so the middle is a little busy.

**At 180 px.** The arms are roughened with turbulence so they look torn like gas, with a colour halo, a glowing core and scattered stars.

**Weaknesses.**
- A swirl is a common logo shape (hurricane, galaxy, pinwheel); it says "nebula" less specifically than the other two.
- The 16 px centre is the muddiest of the three concepts.
- No blue or periwinkle.
