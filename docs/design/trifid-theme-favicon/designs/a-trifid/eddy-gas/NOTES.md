# Eddy gas

**Idea.** One continuous curl of cloud that winds once round a dark eye and thins to a tail; its colour changes along the curl from salmon at the core through cream gold to teal at the tail. The dark channel that follows the curl is the dust lane. On the dark rounded tile.

**At 16 px.** A single fat curl about 14 px across with a 1 px sky margin, three colour areas (salmon, cream, teal) with short blended joins, and a channel of roughly 1 px curling into the eye. No blur, no marbling.

**At 32 and 180 px.** The edge is feathered, the colour joins are blurred into each other, slate sits on the shadow side of the tail, pale highlights on the outer side, and faint dust threads run along the curl. At 180 px turbulence roughens it and there are seven stars of mixed sizes.

**Weaknesses.**
- It can read as a wave, a shell or a letter-like curl (a 6 or an @) rather than a nebula.
- At 16 px the dark eye is 2 px, slightly off the pixel grid, so its edge is a little grey.
- The slate is faint; at 16 px it is absent.
