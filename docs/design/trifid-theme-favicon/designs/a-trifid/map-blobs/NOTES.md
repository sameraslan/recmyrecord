# Map blobs

**Idea.** The album map boiled down: its own lumpy outline with red on top, gold right, blue left, teal at the bottom tail and a periwinkle shoulder, the colours folding into each other along curved seams and brightening towards the middle. It floats with no tile, with a thin sky-coloured rim so it holds on a light tab bar.

**At 16 px.** Four colour areas (periwinkle dropped) inside an uneven outline about 14 px across, with a rim just under a pixel wide. The outline is the only one of the three that is not a rounded square, so it stands out in a tab row; the colours are clear but the outline softens towards a rounded gem.

**At 180 px.** Opaque sky, the seams blurred into each other, the rim roughened with turbulence, a colour halo and stars. This is the closest of the three to the real map.

**Weaknesses.**
- Four colours in 14 px is the limit; on a low-density screen the middle goes pale peach where red meets gold.
- The meaning depends on having seen the map. Without it, it is a pretty multicolour blob.
- On a dark tab bar the rim disappears (fine), on a light one the rim looks slightly heavy at 32 px.
