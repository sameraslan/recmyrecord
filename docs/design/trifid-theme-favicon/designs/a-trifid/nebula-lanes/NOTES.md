# Nebula lanes

**Idea.** The map's uneven cloud, cut by a thin S-shaped dust lane with one branch, into three unequal lobes: a large salmon one, a smaller cream-gold one and a teal one, with slate blue and periwinkle marbled in. No outline; the edge feathers into the dark tile.

**At 16 px.** Hard-edged cloud filling most of the tile, lanes drawn as whole dark pixels (1 px), three colour areas plus a small slate patch on the left. No feather, no marbling blur.

**At 32 px and up.** The edge is feathered, slate, periwinkle and cream patches are blurred into the lobes, and the lane varies in width. At 180 px turbulence roughens the cloud and lanes, a dead-end spur of dust appears and stars are added.

**Tile or no tile.** Both were rendered. Without the tile the feathered pastel edge dissolves into a light tab bar, so the tile was kept.

**Weaknesses.**
- At 16 px the lane and branch still meet as a rough Y, so some three-sector reading remains, though the lobes are unequal and the junction is off-centre.
- At 16 px the cloud nearly fills the tile, so the uneven outline is mostly lost at that size.
- At 32 px the lanes can read as cracks rather than dust.
