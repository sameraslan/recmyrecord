# Three lobes

**Idea.** The Trifid itself: one glowing cloud cut into three lobes (red-orange top, gold right, teal left) by a dark Y of dust, with a single star where the lanes meet. It sits on the dark rounded tile so the dust lanes are simply the sky showing through.

**At 16 px.** Three flat colour blocks about 5 px each, separated by a lane that is a full dark pixel wide. No star, no rim bumps, the lane arms are nearly straight. It reads as a clear three-colour trefoil on both tab bars.

**At 180 px.** The rim is roughened with turbulence, a soft halo of each colour bleeds into the sky, and stars sit in the gas.

**Weaknesses.**
- At 16 and 32 px the lobes are smooth enough that it can read as a tidy three-part logo (a little like a beach ball) rather than gas. The gas feeling only arrives at 180 px.
- It is still a dark rounded tile, so the outline in a tab row is the same as many icons; the colour does the work.
- No blue or periwinkle, so two of the five Ember families are missing.
