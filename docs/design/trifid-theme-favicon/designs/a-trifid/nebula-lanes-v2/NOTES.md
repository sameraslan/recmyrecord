# Nebula lanes v2

**Idea.** The map's uneven cloud on the dark tile, cut by soft dust lanes into three unequal lobes (large salmon, cream gold, teal) with slate blue and periwinkle marbled in. No outline; the edge feathers into the dark.

**At 16 px.** One diagonal lane of whole dark pixels between salmon and cream that fades out at its lower end, plus a small dark notch at the lower right rim. Teal meets the other two by colour alone, so there is no T or Y junction. A slate patch sits on the left. 1 px of sky all round.

**At 32 and 180 px.** The lanes are blurred and built from overlapping strokes of different widths, so they swell and thin like dust. The left patch is now an opaque slate blue instead of a purple mix.

**Weaknesses.**
- At 32 px the lanes are still fairly sharp and meet in a Y; some cracked-shell reading remains at that size.
- At 16 px, with a single lane, it is closer to a three-colour blob than to a cloud with lanes.
- The 16 px and 32 px versions differ more than in the other concepts (one lane against a branching one).
