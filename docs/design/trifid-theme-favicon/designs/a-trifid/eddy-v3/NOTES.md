# Eddy v3

**Idea.** Three gas streams (pale teal, cream gold, salmon) of unequal length curling into a shared cream core, on the dark rounded tile. From 32 px up each arm carries lighter and darker streaks and the colours bleed into each other.

**At 16 px.** The swirl sits inside columns and rows 1 to 14, so there is sky all round. The arms merge into a cream glow at the centre with one cream core pixel; dark channels of about 1 px remain only in the outer part.

**At 32 and 180 px.** Blurred streaks inside each arm (a pale highlight and a shadow in a neighbouring tone), a wider colour halo and a soft core. At 180 px the arms are torn with turbulence and there are seven stars of mixed sizes.

**Weaknesses.**
- Still three arms round a centre, so a pinwheel reading is reduced, not gone.
