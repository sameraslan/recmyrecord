# Eddy v2

**Idea.** The same whirl of three gas streams curling into a small core, redrawn in the map's dustier tones (pale teal, cream gold, salmon) with arms of unequal length so the outline is uneven, not a disc. It stays on the dark rounded tile.

**At 16 px.** Three arms about 2.5 px wide with roughly 1 px dark channels between them, filling about 14 px of the tile. No white core. The arm tips reach close to the tile edge.

**At 32 px and up.** A slight blur softens the arm edges and a wider halo lets the colours bleed into the channels. A small off-centre white core appears. At 180 px the arms are torn with turbulence and stars are added.

**Tile or no tile.** Both were rendered. Without the tile the cream arm and the dark channels wash out on a light tab bar and the 16 px mark goes grey, so the tile was kept.

**Weaknesses.**
- Still a swirl, so some spinner or hurricane association remains.
- The channels at 16 px are near 1 px but not locked to the pixel grid, so they are slightly grey in places.
- No blue or periwinkle.
