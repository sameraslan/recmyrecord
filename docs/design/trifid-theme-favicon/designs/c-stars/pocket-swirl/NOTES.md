# Pocket Swirl

**Idea.** A pocket of the map as five or six stars with no lines, shrinking along a curl from a big red-orange star through gold, teal and periwinkle to a small blue tail. The curl is the theme's swirl, and the flat colours float with no tile, so it is the one icon in the tab row that is not a dark square or circle.

**At 16 px.** Five solid shapes snapped to the grid: red r 4, gold r 2, teal and periwinkle r 1.5, and a 2 px blue square for the tail (the sixth dot is dropped). Reads as a bright red dot with a spray of coloured dots; the colours stay distinct on light and dark.

**Tile.** None, transparent. Gold and teal are slightly deepened (`#f0ae5a`, `#45b4cc`) so they hold on a light tab bar; blue is the weakest on dark but still visible. The 180 px icon puts the same stars on the sky with a soft glow and gas.

**Weaknesses.** Flat dots have no glow, so in the tab it shows the palette but not the nebula. A big dot with smaller dots above can be read as a paw print or a generic "dots" logo; the long tail reduces this but does not remove it. The tab icon and the glowing phone icon are less alike than in the other two concepts.
