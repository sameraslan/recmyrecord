# Ember Trio

**Idea.** Today's mark (three stars joined by lines, one larger) kept exactly, but each star now lights its own cloud: red-orange, cyan-teal and gold. The three clouds meet in the tile like the three lobes of the Trifid.

**At 16 px.** Dark rounded tile, three flat-ish colour areas, 1 px lines on the pixel grid, stars at r 1.5 and r 2.5 in star white. Reads as a small tricolour tile with a white "V" of dots. The lines survive but carry little; colour does the work.

**Tile.** Dark rounded tile (the gas needs black to glow). Checked on light and dark tab bars; the saturated corners keep the tile edge visible on dark.

**Weaknesses.** Busiest of the three: three colours plus lines plus dots is at the limit for 16 px. The gold cloud goes slightly brown where it fades into black. The triangle of nodes still says "graph" as much as "sky". `feTurbulence` warps the gas in the 180 px icon only.
