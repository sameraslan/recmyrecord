# star-r

**Idea.** The same italic r in the site's off-white, but its ball terminal is a star: a white-hot four-point sparkle in a gold-to-ember halo, with the arm warming to gold as it reaches it. A letter and an album-star in one mark.

**At 16 px.** The sparkle points are dropped; the star is a 4 px white dot inside an ember halo about 9 px wide, beside a 2.6 px off-white stem. Reads as "r with a glowing dot", highest contrast of the three on both tab bars.

**Weaknesses.** Only two gas colours (ember and gold, plus a faint blue corner), so it carries less of the map's range. At 16 px the star and the ball are the same thing, so the "it is a star" idea only lands from 32 px up. At 32 px the sparkle is large against the letter and reaches near the tile edge.

Files are generated by `../build.py`.
