# star-eddy

**Idea.** One round off-white star sitting in a curl of gas, like the bright teal knot on the map. The curl is a single tapering arm that starts dusty teal beside the star, turns cream-gold and trails off as a salmon tail to the lower left; a short slate-periwinkle wisp sits inside it at 32 px and up. No letters, no sparkle.

**At 16 px.** Redrawn: a 3 px core, a fatter curl spanning about 13 px, and only two colour areas (teal over the top and right, cream-salmon tail). The inner wisp is dropped because it read as a mouth under the star. Dark tile kept: the pale gas colours wash out on a light tab bar without it; on a dark bar the tile edge fades and the curl carries the mark.

**Weaknesses.** A swirl around a dot is a familiar shape; the warm tail and off-centre star keep it away from the blue browser swirl, but it is still a swirl in a dark rounded square. The core is small at 16 px (it reads as a bright dot, not obviously a star). The 180 px icon uses `feTurbulence` displacement for the marbled edge, so ship it as a PNG render.

Files are generated by `../build3.py` (`arch -x86_64 python3 build3.py`).
