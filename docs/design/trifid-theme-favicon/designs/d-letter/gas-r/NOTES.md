# gas-r

**Idea.** The first letter of the wordmark, a lowercase italic serif r drawn as outlines, filled with the gas itself: teal at the foot, through periwinkle and gold, to ember red at the ball. It sits on the dark sky tile with a faint blue and ember haze in opposite corners.

**At 16 px.** Separate drawing: 2.6 px stem, a one-pixel entry serif, a fat arm and a 4 px ball, 10 degree slant. The gradient is cut to four stops so the stem reads cool and the ball reads warm; that two-colour split is what you actually see in a tab.

**Weaknesses.** The tile edge nearly disappears on a dark tab bar, so the letter floats (still legible, the colours are bright). The mid-stem periwinkle is the dimmest part. It is still a dark tile like many neighbours; the colour of the letter does the distinguishing.

Files are generated by `../build.py` (run with `arch -x86_64 python3 build.py`, PIL on this machine is an Intel build).
