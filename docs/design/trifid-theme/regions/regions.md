# Map regions for the balanced layout

## Method
Feature table `all_data_norm.pkl` (SHA-256 matched the pipeline's pin; 4,081 rows after URI de-duplication, all titles line up with `albums.json`). Four approaches were compared on the balanced layout: (A) k-means on the 2D positions at k = 10/14/20, (B) mean-shift density blobs at bandwidth 0.12/0.16, (C) k-means in the balanced feature space (13 audio + 120 descriptors / 1.765^3) and (D) k-means on descriptors alone, each scored by whether a region has a word with coverage >= 35% and lift >= 1.8x and by how spatially contiguous it is (share of each album's 15 nearest map neighbours in the same group). A and B are contiguous by construction (0.91-0.96) and 23 of 25 mean-shift blobs already had a qualifying word; feature-space clusters say the same things but are scattered on the map (contiguity 0.67 for C, 0.40 for D), so they cannot be drawn as places. The final regions are the 25 mean-shift blobs (bandwidth 0.12), with adjacent blobs that share leading words merged by hand, one blob with no usable word left unnamed (classic-rock middle: playful 36%, 1.7x) and the 18-album spoken-word islet ignored. Each region is then trimmed to its interior (>= 70% of 15 nearest neighbours in the same region, sparse stragglers and the farthest 8% dropped), so 918 of 4,081 albums (22%) are deliberately unnamed in-between territory. Coverage = share of the region's albums that carry the word in the full table (not only the top-10 words shipped in `albums.json`); lift = coverage / share across all albums; "next region" = highest coverage of that word in any other region. Strength: strong = coverage >= 40%, lift >= 1.8x and next region <= 0.8x this one; fair = coverage >= 35% and lift >= 1.8x; audio-named regions are strong at |z| >= 1.5.

Stability: the same albums are located on the sonic and mood layouts. "in one blob" = share of members within 1.25x the region's balanced 90th-percentile radius (rescaled) of their new median position; "spread" = median pairwise distance relative to balanced after rescaling. A region "holds" when >= 70% stay in one blob, spread <= 1.6x and at least half of each member's nearest neighbours are still members.

## Verdict
The idea works on the balanced layout, better than the "it is not purely mood" worry suggests: 17 compact regions: 10 strong on a descriptor word, 2 strong on an audio trait, 5 fair, none that had to be faked. But the honest names are only partly "mood". The strongest are really style words the descriptor set happens to contain (urban, improvisation, progressive, aggressive, raw). The true mood names that hold up are sombre, warm, playful, lonely and ethereal (strong), then bittersweet and pastoral (fair).

- The two owner-favourite words are both fair, not strong. "Bittersweet" covers 56% of its region at 2.6x, but the pastoral region next door carries it just as often (58%), so it describes the whole south-east of the map rather than one place. "Pastoral" has the best lift of the mood words (5.7x) and is exclusive to its region, but only 39% of albums there carry it; "acoustic" (48%, acousticness z +1.5) is the truer description.
- Weakest names: Eclectic Cloud (eclectic 38%; its most common word, psychedelic 45%, is more common in the ethereal region), Hypnotic Orbit (hypnotic 39%, ethereal region has 41%; what really defines it is instrumental 81%), and Epic Expanse (a large merged metal/hard-rock area, radius about twice the others; "heavy" is 78% there but 98% in Aggressive Rift). "Quirky Way" would be honest only as a secondary name: quirky is 38% / 3.7x in the playful region against playful 61%.
- Two regions have no mood identity at all and are named from audio: live recordings (liveness z +3.2, only 5.9 descriptors per album) and the quiet instrumental/classical/soundtrack corner (loudness z -2.1, acousticness +1.8).
- Names do not survive the slider. Each stop is a separate UMAP, so only the islands hold: Live Belt, Urban Cluster and Quiet Deep hold on sonic; Improv Arm and Aggressive Rift hold on mood (Urban, Epic and Sombre are near misses there). Every central mood region (bittersweet, pastoral, lonely, warm, playful, ethereal, eclectic, progressive) scatters to 2-4x its size on both other layouts. On sonic, mood words barely form places at all: outside sampling, heavy, aggressive, acoustic and instrumental, almost no word has a neighbourhood where 40% of albums carry it.

## What a real implementation should do
1. Compute regions in the pipeline, per layout (`regions.json` with one entry per stop, or a `regions` key next to each stop in `positions.json`), using the same recipe: mean-shift on that layout's positions, merge, trim, score. Do not morph balanced labels; cross-fade label sets as the slider passes between stops (fade out by mid-way, fade the next set in). Region ids that hold across stops (live, urban, quiet, improv, aggressive) can keep one label that moves with its centroid.
2. Expect different vocabularies per stop: sonic regions should be named mostly from audio traits (live, quiet, acoustic, loud/heavy, instrumental, spoken), mood regions from mood words (there, sad, soothing, calm, peaceful, anthemic, quirky and summer start to form places that are too diffuse to name on balanced).
3. Gate names in code with the thresholds above (coverage >= 40%, lift >= 1.8x, next region <= 0.8x) and add a pipeline validation test, so a label disappears instead of lying when the catalogue or layout changes. Because mean-shift ids and the merges were chosen by eye, a rebuild that changes the layout needs the merge step redone or replaced by automatic merging on profile similarity.
4. Keep an owner-editable name table keyed by the data word (word -> approved place name), so the adjective stays data-backed while the noun is a copy decision.
5. Draw labels at (cx, cy) with size by n, and leave the trimmed in-between albums unlabelled. Hulls are convex and a few overlap slightly at the edges (Epic Expanse with Aggressive Rift and Progressive Spiral); use them as soft glows, not hard borders.
6. Show strong regions by default; treat the five fair ones as optional or show them at lower emphasis. A tooltip such as "61% of albums here are tagged playful" would make the claim checkable.

Caveats: descriptor presence is RYM-style tagging, so absence of a word is weaker evidence than presence; live albums are thinly tagged. Stability thresholds are my own; the raw numbers are in `regions.json` under `stability`.
