One item is a FAIL: 16.11, a picked album must land above the phone's card. Correction, 2026-10-07: it is a fault that `origin/main` also has, not a regression of the theme. `CameraBounds` pulls the idle camera back onto the cloud 500 ms after the last move and treats the whole viewport as visible; on a phone the slider panel and the card cover the bottom of it, so an album at the cloud's lower edge is eased under them. Measured on a build of `origin/main`: Milestones is centred at about 1.1 s after the pick and then slides down to the same resting place, under the card. The baseline picture shows the cover whole only because of when it was shot: the baseline's capture waited 300 ms after the camera stopped, before the pull starts, and the theme's capture also waits for the sharper gas image (about a second), after it. The pair compared two moments, not two behaviours. The capture script's tap that did not select has the same cause and also happens on `origin/main`. Fixed since, 2026-10-07: the fix has landed on the branch (`136ed758`, then `80eca19f`; `state/bounds.ts`, `canvas/CameraBounds.tsx`), with unit and phone e2e tests. On a phone the camera may rest lower by the height of the panels covering the bottom of the screen, capped so the lowest album rests no higher than the middle of the visible band; the cap protects a phone on its side, where the uncapped change rested the camera on empty sky. Desktop is unchanged. `origin/main` still has the fault. The walk's pictures for this item were not retaken after the fix.
