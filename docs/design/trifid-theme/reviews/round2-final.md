# Round two, revised: final review

Fresh read of all 24 current PNGs under `round2/` against `BRIEF2.md`, `REVISE2.md` and `regions/colour.md`. Designer NOTES not read. Contrast judged by eye. No close-up crops were made this pass.

## Headline
- The main round-two failure is fixed in the space lanes: fresh 02, fresh 01 and darksite 01 no longer read as an island. Fresh 02's overview is now the best single image of the project and clearly beats both round-one favourites.
- Album views are good across the board: lines are visible and consistently drawn everywhere, covers are spread, selection areas are calm.
- New weakness: two lanes over-corrected and lost their colour (galaxy 02 is near monochrome, fresh 01 is mostly grey), and the two nebula concepts converged into one picture.
- Fit-to-width hides three strong regions (The Live Belt, The Quiet Deep, Improv Arm) and usually Hypnotic Orbit. Only the illustrated lane tells the user they exist (small "↑ THE LIVE BELT" / "↓ IMPROV ARM" pointers). Every other concept should borrow that.

## Per concept

### fresh 02 Trifid
- Fixed: yes, fully. No silhouette; rose, gold, blue, violet and teal run to all edges and under the header. Rule line shown. Evidence note is plain text under the name. Fair regions are small italics with no sub-line. Cover 6 no longer touches the seed.
- Colour: the richest of the nine and still reads as painted sky; it sits just short of too saturated (the pink at 430-640, 120-330 and the blue at 0-300, 520-900).
- Honest: Sombre Void blue, Raw Flare rose, Warm Halo gold, Urban violet, Progressive Spiral on a dark neutral band, all correct. Two problems: (a) saturated violet (x > 1450, y 80-560) and blue (x < 250) carry on to the edges where there are no albums, though the brief says far-field gas must be neutral; (b) black ink-like dust lanes (640-760, 420-540 over "Eclectic Cloud"; 880-1240, 380-800 across the gold) sit on top of populated areas, so "dark" looks like "empty" or like a fifth meaning.
- Labels: best lettering; "Eclectic Cloud" (690-800, 466) is lost on a black blot.
- Album views: both strong. Lines have a teal-tinted casing, and teal is now a family colour; in album-b they cross teal gas (1000-1250, 440-640) and survive only because of the white core.
- Fit to width: good trade here. Loses Live Belt, Quiet Deep, Improv Arm, Hypnotic Orbit with no pointer.

### darksite 01 Rho Ophiuchi
- Fixed: mostly. Cloud spans roughly x 190-1390 with brown dust to every edge; no outline. Sombre Void is now a white label on dark blue-grey. Tooltip plate replaced by plain text. Lines to 1 and 5 separated; hover card moved clear.
- Remaining continuity gap: x > 1400 and x < 180 hold only faint dust, so there is still a hint of "cloud in the middle", much weaker than before.
- Colour: rose, violet, gold, blue, teal, with real dark. The bottom third (y 700-1000) lines up blue, pink, teal and yellow in soft vertical stripes, which reads as a pastel rainbow.
- Honest: Aggressive Rift (200-400, 240-330) is lilac-mauve, near Urban Cluster's violet (1150-1380, 150-420), the known rose + blue collision. Everything else agrees with colour.md; Ethereal Veil pale, Progressive Spiral neutral.
- Labels: coloured italic names plus small-caps sub-line for strong, plain smaller italics for fair. Clean. Small-caps sub-lines still catch a few stars.
- Album views: both calm and clear. Album-b is quieter in colour (olive, grey-teal).
- Phone frames: fine.
- Fit to width: good trade; needs off-screen pointers.

### nebula 02 Hubble Palette
- Fixed: yes. Neon slabs gone; labels heavier with star hold-back; rule line shown; album lines white and clear; album-b (teal, A Love Supreme) is beautiful.
- Honest: the far-field smoke is teal-grey everywhere (for example 0-250, 250-1000 and 1300-1600, 300-1000). Teal means quiet in the rule, so the empty sky makes a claim. The Bittersweet Reach (850-1150, 400-620) is olive-khaki, not gold. Ethereal Veil has a confident teal-blue patch (600-720, 490-570) where it should be pale and mixed.
- Continuity: grey-teal smoke to all edges; the coloured cloud is still a shape inside it (x 230-1470) but with no rim.
- Labels: fair regions in very small tracked caps: clearly distinguished, but "HYPNOTIC ORBIT" (478-622, 879) and "EPIC EXPANSE" (448-563, 250) are near the legibility floor.
- Fit to width: partial fit; keeps Hypnotic Orbit, loses Live Belt, Quiet Deep, Improv Arm. Reasonable.

### nebula 01 Cosmic Cliffs
- Fixed: mostly. No longer orange and blue only; grey neutral smoke reaches all edges; album views are calm with mint lines.
- Not fixed: the top (300-1050, 80-340) is still saturated orange-red. Fierce is rendered orange-red while the caption says "rose", and Playful Way's orange-gold next to it is hard to tell apart. An orange edge runs down the right side of the gold (1150-1250, 400-720), a faint rim.
- Honest: same Ethereal Veil teal patch as nebula 02. Neutral grey far field is correct here (better than 02).
- Overview layout, labels and shapes are the same as nebula 02; only the tints differ.

### fresh 01 Remnant
- Fixed: yes for neon, continuity and selection calm. Smoke fills the whole frame; no island.
- Cost: colour. The frame is about two-thirds grey smoke; family colour lives only in bright threads. Gold is the only family that carries.
- Labels: thin caps over busy threads; "The Bittersweet Reach" (890-1080, 705) and "Pastoral Nebula" (950-1085, 970) are crossed by bright filaments. Evidence note in plain text is good.
- Album: calm centre, but white lines sit near white-gold threads (1250-1600, 150-420) and lines to 1, 2 and 4 are short.
- Fit to width: good trade; no pointers.

### illustrated 02 Airbrush (new)
- Soft grainy airbrush colour, no outlines; shared colours and rule line; off-screen pointers. A fair replacement for Overprint.
- Labels: wide embossed display face. Stars overprint it ("Urban Cluster" spike at 1272, 208; dots through "Raw Flare" 530-710, 98). "Pastoral Nebula" collides with "Lonely Drift" (985-1105, 778). Short underline dashes under each name are decoration.
- Honest: good; Progressive Spiral and Ethereal Veil pale. Aggressive Rift lavender (300-450, 150-350) again nears Urban violet.
- Album: lines 6, 7 and 8 leave in one direction (1065-1430, 300-480) and read as a chain; long dotted leaders (1040-1300, 400-520) add clutter; list row 8 is cut by the bottom edge.
- Continuity: funnel of colour on dark plum; soft, but the lower-left and right corners are empty.

### galaxy 01 Long Exposure colour
- Fixed: album lines (all white, cased), cover spacing, stains, label weight. Labels are now the most legible of the nine.
- Not fixed: continuity. A funnel-shaped glow on flat charcoal with empty areas at x < 150 below y 400 and x > 1250 below y 350. Album view is black beyond x 1300.
- Colour: pale pastel wash; less colourful than either round-one favourite.
- Large soft star blooms (for example 925, 543; 835, 577) vary a lot in size; if size is not rank this is decoration read as data.
- Honest: good. Three-line caption is longer than the one quiet line asked for.

### illustrated 01 Gouache
- Fixed: contour rims and island outlines are gone; colour is one field of short strokes that mixes between regions, which shows blends honestly. Has off-screen pointers.
- Continuity: the stroke field carries to all edges in navy, but the coloured funnel is still a distinct shape (x 280-1400).
- Album: confetti of pink, orange and violet strokes directly behind the covers (700-1500, 100-900); Warm Halo reads orange-pink-violet rather than gold; leaders for covers 4 and 8 tangle (1195-1340, 690-750); list rows 8 and 9 run off the bottom.
- Labels: large script hides stars; charming but not a sky.

### galaxy 02 Named nebulae
- Fixed: rainbow and fog are gone; album lines and spacing are good.
- Over-corrected: grey-white haze with colour only in small knots (Urban 1300-1420, 110-300; Lonely Drift 750-1100, 850-960; Playful 840-960, 200-380). The caption promises five colours the picture barely shows. Fails "colourful".
- Continuity: same funnel on black as galaxy 01.

- nebula 01 and nebula 02: same overview layout, shapes and labels; only tint differs. Keep 02 for the softer fierce colour and the teal album-b, but give it 01's neutral grey far field.
- galaxy 01 and illustrated 02: both soft pastel airbrush funnels. Keep one at most.
- galaxy 01 and galaxy 02: same engine; 02 is 01 with the colour removed. Drop 02.
- fresh 02 and darksite 01: alike in palette; fine to show both as the top pair.

## Should not be shown as-is
- galaxy 02, overview and album: nearly monochrome; contradicts "colourful".
- nebula 01 overview alongside nebula 02 overview: the owner would see the same image twice; show one. If 01 is shown, its orange-red top contradicts the "rose" caption.
- illustrated 01 album and illustrated 02 album: the closest-albums list is cut off at the bottom edge (rows 8-9), which looks like a bug.
- galaxy 01 overview: can be shown only as a labelling reference; it still has the empty-corner funnel.
- fresh 02 overview is the lead image, but before it is called final the black dust blots over populated areas and the saturated violet and blue at the empty edges should be toned down.
