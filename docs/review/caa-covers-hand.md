# Cover Art Archive covers matched by hand

The automatic lookup of `rmr_pipeline/covers.py` (the last resort) takes a MusicBrainz release group only when its title, one artist and its year all match. It left 40 albums without a cover (`still-no-cover.md`). On 6 October 2026 a reader (Claude, in a Claude Code session) searched MusicBrainz for each of them through its web service, and for "West Side Story", whose automatic match the first review had found wrong (no. 25 of `caa-covers.jpg`). This file is the record: album, release group, why it is the same album, and what the rule had objected to.

**State: finished.** 34 release groups were chosen; 30 of them now give their album a cover.

- The Cover Art Archive answered HTTP 503 to every request from 23:16 that evening to at least 23:33; it answered again at 23:51, and `scripts/caa_hand.py` then asked for the 34 groups' lists of images (35 requests, none refused).
- 32 groups have a front image and are in `data-pipeline/catalog/covers_caa.csv` with `hand` in `matched_by`; their 32 sprites were made.
- 2 groups have no front image and were not added: e2c73b4e (Van Cliburn, "Concerto No. 1") and 67458b47 (Sadao Watanabe, "Super Mario World").
- A reader looked at each of the 32 sprites. 30 show the album's sleeve. 2 are on `covers_skip.csv`: for Wii Sports (430fb2a6) and A Link to the Past (482e30a7) the archive serves, as the group's front image, the sleeve of a bootleg game rip (MusicBrainz releases 1d85ba6a, "Wii Sports Original Soundtrack", and 27ac7f0c, "The Legend of Zelda - A Link to the Past", both with the status Bootleg), a fan-made image. The official Nintendo Music releases of both groups have an image of their own on the archive (8e02ca4d and d3052a09; da9ddcbd and 4ae0323b), but the `caa` tier addresses a release group, not a release.

The 4 albums are in `still-no-cover.md` with the 7 not matched below. The 34 rows stay in `caa-covers-hand-pending.csv` as the record of what was chosen (a rerun of the script asks only for the 2 groups without an image). The steps were, from `data-pipeline/`:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/caa_hand.py                # adds each pending row whose group has a front image
.venv/bin/python -m rmr_pipeline.covers sprites
.venv/bin/python -m rmr_pipeline.build --require-sprites --map-root <map root>
.venv/bin/python -m rmr_pipeline.validate
.venv/bin/python scripts/caa_review.py              # then look at the new cells of caa-covers.jpg
```

West Side Story's wrong group (ccdcf53e) stays on `covers_skip.csv`; its row of `covers_caa.csv` now names dd5d8373, whose image is the 1961 soundtrack's red sleeve.

## The searches

91 requests to `musicbrainz.org` (release-group and release searches, and lookups of a group's releases or a release's tracks), at most one a second, with the module's fetcher and User-Agent: 90 answered, 1 was a mistyped release id (HTTP 404); no 503 and no refusal. 18 requests to `coverartarchive.org` between 23:16 and 23:33, each answered HTTP 503 or timed out; then one at 23:51, answered, the 35 of `scripts/caa_hand.py`, the 32 images, and 4 lists of images with 8 MusicBrainz lookups to see which release each doubtful image belongs to.
